# Insurance-Risk-and-Claims-Analysis
🚀 Insurance Risk &amp; Claims Analysis | Data Analytics Project  I recently completed an end-to-end Data Analytics Project focused on Insurance Risk &amp; Claims Analysis using Excel, Power BI,  and DAX.

📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes insurance policy and claims data to uncover risk patterns, customer behavior, and claim trends. The goal was to transform raw Kaggle dataset data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, modeling, visualization, and reporting.

📂 Dataset
• Source: Kaggle Dataset (CSV File)
• Domain: Insurance & Risk Analytics
• Data Included: Policyholder demographics, claim amounts, vehicle details, education, marital status, and coverage zones.

🛠 Tools & Technologies Used
• Excel – Data loading and initial cleaning
• Power BI – Dashboard development and data visualization
• DAX – KPI calculations and business measures
• Gamma AI – Presentation and report creation

📊 Dashboard Highlights
✔ Total Policies Analysis
✔ Claim Amount & Claim Frequency Tracking
✔ Gender-Based Policy Distribution
✔ Vehicle Usage & Coverage Zone Analysis
✔ Age Group Risk Segmentation
✔ Education & Marital Status Claim Analysis
✔ Vehicle Year Trend Analysis
✔ Interactive KPI and Filter-Based Dashboard

📈 Key Results & Insights
• Analyzed 37,542 insurance policies with total claims of $187.8M
• Identified Single policyholders as the highest claim-risk segment
• Private vehicle policies represented the majority of the portfolio
• Young drivers had lower policy counts but higher risk potential
• Education level showed a strong relationship with claim behavior
• Geographic coverage zones were evenly diversified across regions

✅ Conclusion
This project strengthened my skills in data cleaning, DAX calculations, dashboard storytelling, and business insight generation. It also improved my ability to convert raw data into actionable insights for decision-making.
